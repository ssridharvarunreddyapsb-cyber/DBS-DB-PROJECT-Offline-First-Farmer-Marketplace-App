How do I list my produce?

Go to "My Listings" and tap "+ Add Product." Add a photo, name, price, and quantity — that's it.

What if I lose internet while listing?

Keep going — your listing saves on your phone and posts automatically once you're back online.

Is my phone number shared with buyers?

No. Buyers message you inside the app; your number stays private.
